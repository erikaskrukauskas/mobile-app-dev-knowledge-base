# Audio has a text alternative

A video or a voice clip that carries information leaves out anyone who cannot hear it in that moment.

## Rule

Provide captions or a transcript for audio that carries the content. A decorative sound does not need a transcript. Say which is which.

## Specify

- Which clips carry information.
- Where the text alternative lives.
- How a person reaches it.

## Check

Play the clip with the sound off. The information is still available as text. Run it on a mobile client. See [Verify on a mobile client, then record](04-verify-and-record.md).

## Source

Checked 2026-10-19. [WCAG 2 Overview](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/). The page supports this rule: WCAG's perceivable principle requires that information not depend on a single sense. Confirm the current success criterion for audio before you claim conformance.
